# Retention and evidence review

Review the whole editorial chain after drafting. This is an evidence-aware narrative audit, not a formulaic retention score.

## Checks

1. Restate the packaging promise in one sentence and identify where the video earns it.
2. Confirm useful proof appears early enough: result, comparison, demonstration, or concrete consequence.
3. Follow the causal progression. Every beat must create the need for the next beat.
4. Match every `setup_id` to one `closes_setup_id`; flag abandoned promises and unearned reveals.
5. Remove duplicate beats that only restate the same thesis.
6. Verify demonstrations and claims against `REFERENCES.md`; accepted source types are `primary|secondary|local_data|experience|inference|unverified`.
7. Check that proposed visuals prove, clarify, or reset attention rather than decorate narration.
8. Check the word budget against measured WPM when available; label any generic rate as provisional.
9. Make the CTA the earned next action from the video, not an interruption.

Return severity-ranked findings and a setup/closure table. Generic retention percentages are hypotheses unless Artur's channel data supports them. Never invent a citation or convert `unverified` into fact.
